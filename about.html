We Are

The agency was founded in 2010 by Gabi Heininger and is supported by a small, dedicated team.

We have showrooms in Munich and Düsseldorf and represent selected premium and luxury brands in the German and Austrian markets.

Our portfolio comprises shoes, bags, and accessories. The agency's passion is for the exceptional — products with edgy style that tell a story.

At the heart of it are family businesses, young and long-established workshops with distinctive products whose standards of quality, design, and craftsmanship meet the highest benchmarks.

We place great value on long-term partnerships, both with our brands and with our retail partners.

Trust, continuity, and a shared understanding of the market form the core of how we work — always with the goal of achieving healthy, sustainable growth together.

We love what we do……

The Luxury Niche

When Gabi Heininger decided around 12 years ago to go independent with her own showroom, she did so in the knowledge that buyers of fashion and of shoes and accessories simply work differently in their order rhythms and habits. “Unlike fashion collections, orders for shoes and accessories are placed at the trade fairs. That’s where customers come to write orders, so my team and I simply have to be there,” says the agency owner.

With exquisite, largely handmade products from family-run and long-established companies, her brand portfolio relies on continuity and an exclusive niche in which the individual product takes centre stage.

She is constantly searching for new, exclusive products to offer her customers a fresh shopping experience time and again — never losing sight of products that tell a story and whose quality and workmanship meet the highest standards of craftsmanship.
